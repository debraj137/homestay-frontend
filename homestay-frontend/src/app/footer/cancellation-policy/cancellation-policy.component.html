Cancellation & Refund Policy

We understand that plans can change. Our cancellation policy ensures flexibility while also protecting our hosts.

1. Free Cancellation

Guests can cancel within 24 hours of booking for a full refund, provided the check-in date is at least 7 days away.

2. Partial Refunds

- Cancellations made up to 5 days before check-in will receive a 50% refund.
- Cancellations made within 5 days of check-in are non-refundable.

3. No-Show Policy

If a guest does not arrive on the scheduled check-in date, the booking will be considered a no-show and no refund will be issued.

4. Host Cancellations

In the rare event a host cancels a confirmed booking, guests will receive a 100% refund along with priority rebooking support.

5. Refund Timeline

Refunds (if applicable) will be processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

Note: Cancellation terms may vary depending on the host’s individual policy. Always review the booking details before confirming your stay.
